What we did

  1. 1

    Ask why

    What should be different when this works? Who has to do what?

  2. 2

    Find the gap

    Read the data. Talk to sales, success and customers. Find where it's really stuck.

    Pick the channel

    Video, mail, email, an event, a one-pager. Whatever fits the gap, not the habit.

    Write it, design it, shoot it, cut it, ship it. The details matter.

    Measure it
