      Personas, customer journey maps and competitive audits, so the story starts from what people actually care about.

    06 — About

    Hey there, I'm Chris.

    I started in a creative agency with a camera in my hand and never really put it down. Since then I've led marketing in-house at tech companies, which taught me to pair the story with the strategy.

    I work across both worlds: I can direct a shoot, design the campaign, write the copy and build the plan that proves it worked. I'm collaborative, organized to a fault, and happiest when a project has a clear brief and a little room to surprise you.

    Off the clock I host long-table dinners in Easley, South Carolina, pull espresso shots, and look for any excuse to get people around a table.
