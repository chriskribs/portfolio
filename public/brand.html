The notebook · v1

Drawn, not decorated.

The brand is a working notebook. Ruled paper, red pen, tape and notes in the margin. The color and the attitude come from my dad's old band, Chronic Disorder: photocopied flyers, a hand-drawn tape cover and a green you can't miss. It's a nod to where I come from, not a genre.

01 The mark

The split diamond.

C and K cut into a diamond split down the middle, after the Chronic Disorder tape logo. The C is the left half. The K stem runs down the split, and its short arms are cut flush with the diamond's edge, so their ends make the arrow point. Spiky edges, a little drippy. This version is a stand-in until I draw it by hand.

05 Doodles

In the margins.

Small drawings of the work itself: video, sound, results, the people. They draw themselves in as they scroll into view. These are stand-ins until I draw and scan the real ones.

06 Rules

How it works.

  1. One metal word per header. Only on the payoff word. Never in body copy, buttons, the nav or a whole line.
  2. Doodles live in the margins. Never behind text or on top of the work. Two or three per section, max.
  3. Green leads. Acid and red pen are small accents. Red pen is a line, never a fill.
  4. If a marker can't draw it, it doesn't belong. No gradients, glows, bevels or 3D.
  5. One handwritten note per screen. It's the human in the margin, so it has to feel like one.
  6. A tribute, not a genre. No skulls, gore or band imagery. Nothing that says I illustrate for metal bands.
  7. Repeats flip, never rotate. Any pattern made from the mark mirrors across an axis. It never spins into a pinwheel.
  8. The work stays clean. Project images and films never get doodled on, taped over or torn.
07 To draw

The shot list.

Black marker on plain white paper, one drawing per photo, shot straight down in daylight with no shadow. Each one gets cut out and swapped in for its stand-in.

  1. The mark. C + K split diamond, three or four tries, big.
  2. "Chris Kribs" lettered once, chunky, for the nav.
  3. Metal words: did. · vibes. · Chris. · talk · and the brand page words.
  4. Arrows. Five or six, short and long, a couple with a curl.
  5. Underlines. Three, one fast, one double, one wobbly.
  6. Circles. Three loops, the kind you'd draw around a number.
  7. Video: play button, film strip, waveform.
  8. Sound: faders, headphones, drumsticks.
  9. Results: chart going up, lightning bolt, star.
  10. People: heart, envelope, sparkle, a curly arrow.
