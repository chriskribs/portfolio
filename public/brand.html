The notebook · v1

Drawn, not decorated.

The brand is a working notebook. Ruled paper, red pen, tape and notes in the margin. The color and the attitude come from my dad's old band, Chronic Disorder: photocopied flyers, a hand-drawn tape cover and a green you can't miss. It's a nod to where I come from, not a genre.

01 The mark

The split diamond.

C and K cut into a diamond split down the middle, after the Chronic Disorder tape logo. The C is the left half. The K stem runs down the split, and its short arms are cut flush with the diamond's edge, so their ends make the arrow point. Spiky edges, a little drippy. Drawn by hand in black marker, five takes. Take 3 is the mark; the cleanest one, take 5, thickened, is the favicon. Every drawn line on the site boils: three slightly different frames swap about eight times a second, like cheap cel animation.

06 Rules

How it works.

  1. One metal word per header. Only on the payoff word. Never in body copy, buttons, the nav or a whole line.
  2. Doodles live in the margins. Never behind text or on top of the work. Two or three per section, max.
  3. Green leads. Acid and red pen are small accents. Red pen is a line, never a fill.
  4. If a marker can't draw it, it doesn't belong. No gradients, glows, bevels or 3D.
  5. One handwritten note per screen. It's the human in the margin, so it has to feel like one.
  6. A tribute, not a genre. No skulls, gore or band imagery. Nothing that says I illustrate for metal bands.
  7. Repeats flip, never rotate. Any pattern made from the mark mirrors across an axis. It never spins into a pinwheel.
  8. The work stays clean. Project images and films never get doodled on, taped over or torn.
