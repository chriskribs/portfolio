06 · Out of the mark

Every element is already inside the CEK mark. The diagonals are an arrow, and the K stem and the top of the C make a right triangle. Both share the mark's step: two pixels across for every one down.

07 · Elements

The Chevron

Forward motion. It replaces arrows everywhere: buttons, next, the career path, the ticker, and the headline that matters most.

The Notch

The triangle, cut out of the top-right corner in four 2:1 steps. On tags, buttons, cards and photos.

The Flag

The notch filled back in with a second color. It nudges up and out on hover.

Chevron field

Chevrons in formation, for big quiet moments like the contact page. Fade it out with a mask.

Triangle field

Stepped triangles in rows. Background texture on color, in one tone.
