Holland Home passed its three-month goal in three weeks

 

Holland Home set a goal of 60% monthly resident engagement within three months. Both of its Michigan communities passed it in the first three weeks: 71% of independent living residents at Breton Woods and 66% at Raybrook used the app. One resident wrote that it holds "everything I need to know in one place." Three steps drove the result: a structured rollout, in-person training for residents and staff, and resident ambassadors who keep the momentum going.

INTEGRATION OF THE MONTH

PointClickCare: stop entering residents twice

The PointClickCare integration creates resident accounts in Wellzesta and keeps them current from the record your team already maintains. Phone numbers, mailing addresses, and each resident's place in the community update without double entry. PointClickCare stays the source of truth, so each change happens in one place. You choose which PointClickCare sites feed which Wellzesta community.
